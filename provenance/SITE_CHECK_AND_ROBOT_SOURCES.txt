SITE CHECKS AND ROBOT OPTIONS — 10 November 2026

These checklists are authored prompts informed by UK guidance, not an HSE-approved form, a complete risk assessment or evidence that a workplace is safe. They record declarations for an offline plan. Manufacturer descriptions are not independent tests or proof of suitability. No vendors were contacted.

SITE CHECKS
1. HSE: Risk assessment for cleaning
https://www.hse.gov.uk/cleaning/topics/risk-assessment.htm
Identify hazards, decide on controls, put them in place. Grounds the work-area questions.
2. HSE: Steps needed to manage risk
https://www.hse.gov.uk/simple-health-safety/risk/steps-needed-to-manage-risk.htm
Record findings and review controls when circumstances change. Grounds review details and reset-on-change behavior.
3. HSE: Slips and trips in cleaning
https://www.hse.gov.uk/cleaning/topics/slips.htm
Wet surfaces, cables, obstructions and public access need controls; signs alone do not prevent entry.
4. HSE: Control of substances hazardous to health in cleaning
https://www.hse.gov.uk/cleaning/topics/coshh.htm
Assess hazardous substances and exposure; give suitable instruction and training. The app does not choose chemical concentrations or certify compatibility.
5. HSE: Working at height whilst window cleaning
https://www.hse.gov.uk/cleaning/topics/window-cleaning.htm
Site-specific height, access, public location, weather, ground and equipment matter. The app records facade/specialist requests but does not assign an ordinary worker to them.
6. HSE: Musculoskeletal disorders and managing risks in cleaning
https://www.hse.gov.uk/cleaning/backpain.htm
https://www.hse.gov.uk/cleaning/manage-risks.htm
Work methods, physical demands, equipment and training matter. The app does not estimate a worker's health or fatigue.
7. UK Civil Aviation Authority: Drones
https://www.caa.co.uk/drones/
UK flight and operation requirements need an operation-specific review. A US manufacturer's certification statement is not UK permission.

PRODUCT SOURCES

Ozmo — Skyline Robotics
https://www.skylinerobotics.com/
The manufacturer describes robotic exterior-window cleaning using a building maintenance platform.
Proposed checks: Compatible building access platform and installation; Facade geometry, glazing and cleaning method; Exclusion area, trained staff and a site trial.

Kite facade-cleaning robot — Kite Robotics
https://www.kiterobotics.com/en/the-robot/
The manufacturer describes a rotating brush moved across a facade by four cables and winches.
Proposed checks: Building-specific anchors and cable arrangement; Brush access to frames, recesses and facade materials; Weather limits, public protection and cleaning-quality trial.

Sherpa cleaning drone — Lucid Bots
https://www.lucidbots.com/sherpa-drone
The manufacturer describes exterior washing with ground-fed water or solution. A pilot controls the drone and a ground crew supports it.
Proposed checks: Trained pilot, ground crew and local flight permissions; Weather, airspace, hoses and keeping the public clear; Surface/product compatibility, runoff and a test panel; spraying may not replace scrubbing.

KIRA B 50 — Karcher
https://www.karcher.com/gb/en/professional/products/cleaning-robots-c6331
The manufacturer describes robotic scrubbing and drying for medium to large hard-floor areas.
Proposed checks: Floor material, slope, load and water tolerance; Route clearance, steps, low obstacles and people; Staff instruction, servicing and a site cleaning trial.

KIRA CV 50 — Karcher
https://www.karcher.com/gb/en/professional/products/cleaning-robots-c6331
The manufacturer lists robotic vacuum cleaning for medium-sized carpeted and hard-floor areas.
Proposed checks: Dry vacuuming matches the requested cleaning outcome; Floor and route suitability, obstacles and people; Operator instruction, docking and a site cleaning trial.

WINBOT W2 PRO OMNI — ECOVACS
https://www.ecovacs.com/uk/campaign/ces-robot-window-cleaner-winbot
This consumer-facing product page describes a portable window-cleaning robot. It does not establish suitability or permitted commercial use for this job.
Proposed checks: Exact pane dimensions, glass type and permitted use in its manual; Safe placement/retrieval, fall protection and supervision; Cleaning outcome, commercial-use terms and a site trial.

NOT INFERRED
No speed, cost, safety superiority, deployment approval, autonomous drone flight, integration API, availability, procurement price or cleaning performance is established for the user's site. Sherpa is explicitly pilot-operated on its manufacturer's page. No current product match is asserted for desks, whiteboards, wall marks or bin emptying. Those jobs may need a separately designed system and evidence.
