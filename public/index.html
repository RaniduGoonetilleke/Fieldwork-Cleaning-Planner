A QUICK LOOK AROUND

One job at a time.

  1. Describe the job.Name the building, count the windows and add their rough sizes.
  2. Choose the team.Start with the free resources. A robot booking also needs its helper.
  3. Check the work.Record permission, suitability and workplace checks. Leave unknown answers as “Not checked”.
  4. Make your plan.Save a draft or reserve the resources. A second job in the same stage cannot take them again.

A booking reserves resources in this draft. It does not prove a robot can do the job or give permission to start work.

After the work, record the outcome. A supervisor can check the saved note separately.
