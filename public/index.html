Read the walkthrough
  1. Name the building and choose the jobs. The one-click examples use invented answers.
  2. Check who is free and what they are trained to do. This demo has two example people and two stages.
  3. Answer the place and work checks. Leave anything unknown as Not checked.
  4. Record your assessment. The app records what you say; it does not inspect the building.
  5. Read who is assigned and what must wait. Open Why this result? for the reasons and missing robot evidence.
  6. A busy person cannot take another job in the same stage. A failed room check stops all jobs in that room.
  7. Try the separate tested examples for robot options. The G1 example uses recorded simulation evidence and invented current conditions. A free helper still needs to give a separate practice OK.
  8. Download a readable PDF to print or share. Exact JSON remains an optional research export or notebook backup.
  9. Use Robots & results to save new robots and test results. These notes do not change the plans or qualify a robot.
  10. At the bottom of a plan, open After the work and choose Record a job outcome.
  11. Pick the planned job. Record what happened, who did it and whether extra help was needed. Save the outcome.
  12. The supervisor’s acceptance or request for more work is recorded separately on the saved note. The name is typed, not verified.
  13. Changing the outcome removes its old check. It must be checked again. Examples remain labelled as invented and do not count as real work.
  14. The app never starts a robot.
