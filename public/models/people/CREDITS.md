# Clothed people in the team gallery

These two illustrative people use the **Background Posed Humans Pack by Quaternius**. They do not depict the study participants or authors.

- Creator and pack: https://quaternius.com/packs/backgroundposedhumans.html
- Licence: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), verified on the creator's pack page on 6 November 2026.
- Original files: `source/Male_Standing.obj`, `source/Female_Standing.obj`, their material files, and hairstyle 1 for each model.
- Original licence: `source/License.txt`, copied without changes from the creator-linked download folder. Its heading says “Knight Pack” although its credit line says “Background characters”. This is an upstream inconsistency; the pack's own web page independently labels these models CC0.

`worker-man.glb` and `worker-woman.glb` combine each standing model with its hairstyle. Hair was positioned on the head, the models were centred and scaled to 1.75 units tall, and materials were converted to matte PBR. Original material colours were kept. Both files contain all their geometry and materials, with no external textures, animation or skeleton required.

Credit is retained here to make the source clear, although CC0 does not require attribution. The page and files were read as data; no downloaded code was run.
